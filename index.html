Our Same Class Love Story 📚

The First Bench Moment 🪑

Same classroom. Same attendance sheet. I never thought the person sitting just two benches away would become my favorite person in the entire batch.

Library Pretending 📖

We went to the library to "study". But somehow, we ended up talking about everything except the syllabus. And I realised... I didn’t just like sitting beside you. I liked being around you.

Canteen Memories 🍵

Sharing food. Stealing fries. That one chair that somehow always became “our spot”. Small moments. Normal days. But with you, even ordinary felt special.

Hostel Goodbyes 🌙

Even though we’re in the same college, those hostel timings always felt unfair. Every “okay bye” secretly meant, “Stay 5 minutes more.”

Still Choosing You 💛

From classmates… to friends… to something much more beautiful. 11 months later, I still look for you first in every classroom. And I still smile the same way.
